Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Introduction

RestroMind ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your information when you use the RestroMind platform (the "App"). By using the App, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Restaurant details: restaurant name, address, logo and business information.
  • Menu content: dish names, images, descriptions, categories and prices.
  • Support communications and feedback.

b) Information generated through use

  • Order data, preparation times, revenue and sales statistics.
  • Customer order details visible to you for fulfilment, such as customer name and order items.
  • Notification and app preferences.

c) Device and technical information

  • Device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs and usage analytics.
  • Camera or gallery access is used only when you choose to upload images.

d) Sign-in with Google

If you use Continue with Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name and email address.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and provide App features.
  • To process and display orders, menu changes and business statistics.
  • To send order alerts, updates and important service notifications.
  • To improve performance, fix bugs and develop new features.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse or security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only with:

  • Service providers (such as cloud hosting, analytics, notifications and payment partners) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Customers, to the extent needed to fulfil their orders (for example, restaurant name and order status).
  • Authorities or legal bodies when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, we may retain certain data for a limited period for legal, tax, security or dispute-resolution purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls and secure servers, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update your profile and restaurant information in the App at any time.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent or opt out of non-essential notifications via Settings.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Notifications

You can enable or disable push notifications in the Settings screen of the App. Essential service messages, such as security alerts, may still be sent.

9. Cookies and Analytics

We may use analytics tools and similar technologies to understand App usage and improve our services. These tools may collect anonymous or aggregated technical data.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may link to or integrate with third-party services, such as Google Sign-In. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will carry a new "Last updated" date, and significant changes will be notified through the App or email.
